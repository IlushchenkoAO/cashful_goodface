User flow and screens

One login, two account types (Peer / Personal and Developer). Sign-up stays minimal; everything about qualification happens later, inside the dashboard. Boxes are screens, the coloured notes under them are the error, waiting, locked and rejected branches. Every screen is clickable in the prototype: see the screen map.

Error or rejection Waiting (with how long) Locked (with the reason) Success

1. One entry: identify, then log in, link or sign up

Nobody chooses between “Log in” and “Sign up”. They give an email or use a provider; the server decides. The CTA on the site can pass ?type=peer or ?type=developer; it is kept through the whole flow. Log in never asks for an account type: the person lands in the profile they used last.

  1. 01 Log in or sign upGoogle, GitHub, Apple, or email + Continue. A small line shows the CTA’s intent.
    • Rate limited → neutral message, Continue paused with a countdown
    • Network error → Try again
    • Provider cancelled → notice, email still works
  2. →
  3. Account exists?The lookup always takes the same time, known email or not.
  1. 02 Welcome backExisting email. Password, forgot password, or “Email me a login link instead”.
    • Wrong password → attempts left
    • 5 failed attempts → paused 15 minutes, countdown
    • Linked provider → this screen is skipped
  2. →
  3. Two-step checkOnly when 2FA is on
  4. →
  5. DashboardLast used profile. With a Developer CTA from a Personal login, the Developer account is added and opened in the same login.
  1. 06 You already have an accountThe email of the provider already has a password. Log in once to link it.
    • Use a different account → back to 01
  2. →
  3. DashboardBoth ways of signing in work from now on
  1. 03 How do you want to earn?New email, and only when there is no ?type. Personal or Developer.
  2. →
  3. 04 Create accountAccount type chip (Change), email locked, password (not for providers), consent. The country is read on the server.
    • Country not US or EU → a dedicated screen with “Notify me”
  4. →
  5. 05 Check your email6-digit code, 10 minutes. Skipped for provider sign-ups.
    • Wrong or expired code → try again or resend
    • Resend → wait 60 s. “Wrong email? Change” returns to 01.
  6. →
  7. DashboardPeer: “Download the app”. Developer: the details and KYC step first (KYC can wait), then Analytics.

Under 30 seconds, no ID, no payment details, no business data at sign-up.

  1. Verification done at sign-upSubmitted to review

  2. →
  3. ApprovedAnalytics shows data. Apps, SDK download and Payouts open.

Two-factor authentication is only asked when logging in (and only if it is on). Nothing inside the dashboard asks for it.
